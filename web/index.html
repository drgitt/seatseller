SeatSeller

Turn on notifications to hear about games that are selling fast.
Market low is the cheapest seat in the whole arena, so your section costs more. For a real row price, open a marketplace link on a game, filter to your section, and type the low, high and seat count here. Selling fast appears when your listed seats drop sharply in a week, the market low jumps, or a marketplace shows low inventory.

What you type stays on this phone.

Order and alerts

If notifications are late or missing, tap Allow background activity and choose Unrestricted or Don't optimize for SeatSeller. Then run the self-test.

Selling-fast rules

These control the red Selling fast tag in the app. Phone notifications use the alerts from the daily price check.

Seller fees

Estimates only. Each marketplace sets its own seller fee and changes it, so check yours and edit these numbers. They drive the "nets about" amounts and your profit totals.

Reminders

Reminders skip games you marked sold. Game times aren't in the schedule, so they fire at 9 AM. Android may deliver them a few minutes late.

Calendar

Adds an all-day event for each upcoming game with a list-by reminder. Run it again after you add games and it updates what it made.

Backup

Backups save your teams, prices, sales and settings.
